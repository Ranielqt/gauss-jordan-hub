Project Part I

The Mechanics of Gauss-Jordan Elimination

Gauss–Jordan elimination is an extension of standard Gaussian elimination. While the latter seeks to transform a matrix into Row Echelon Form (REF), Gauss-Jordan continues the process until the matrix reaches Reduced Row Echelon Form (RREF).

The core objective is to apply Elementary Row Operations—swapping rows, multiplying by non-zero scalars, and adding multiples of rows together—until the coefficient portion of the augmented matrix becomes the Identity matrix \(I\).

"For a system \(Ax = b\), the RREF of \([A|b]\) provides the solution vector directly in the final column."

Elementary Operations

  • 01.

    Row Swapping

    Interchange two rows to bring a non-zero element to the pivot position.

  • 02.

    Scalar Multiplication

    Multiply a row by a non-zero constant to normalize the pivot element to 1.

  • 03.

    Row Addition

    Add a multiple of one row to another to produce zeros in the pivot's column.

Example I: 2x2 Basic System Status: Direct Solution

The Problem

Solve for \(x\) and \(y\) using row normalization and elimination:

\[ \begin{cases} 2x + y = 8 \\ 3x - y = 7 \end{cases} \]
1

Form Augmented Matrix

\[ \left( \begin{array}{cc|c} 2 & 1 & 8 \\ 3 & -1 & 7 \end{array} \right) \]

Capture coefficients into a table.

2

Normalize Row 1 (\(R_1 = \frac{1}{2}R_1\))

\[ \left( \begin{array}{cc|c} 1 & 0.5 & 4 \\ 3 & -1 & 7 \end{array} \right) \]

Scale pivot \(a_{11}\) to 1.

3

Eliminate Below (\(R_2 = R_2 - 3R_1\))

\[ \left( \begin{array}{cc|c} 1 & 0.5 & 4 \\ 0 & -2.5 & -5 \end{array} \right) \]

Zero out the column under the first pivot.

4

Normalize Row 2 (\(R_2 = \frac{R_2}{-2.5}\))

\[ \left( \begin{array}{cc|c} 1 & 0.5 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{array} \right) \]

Final normalization for the second variable.

5

Eliminate Above (\(R_1 = R_1 - 0.5R_2\))

\[ \left( \begin{array}{cc|c} 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{array} \right) \]

Reaching reduced row echelon form (RREF).

Final Solution Vector
x = 3, y = 2
Example II: 3x3 Advanced System Status: Multi-Step Elimination

The Problem

Solve for \(x, y,\) and \(z\) using an elimination sequence:

\[ \begin{cases} x + y + z = 6 \\ 2y + 5z = -4 \\ 2x + 5y - z = 27 \end{cases} \]
1

Initial Augmented Matrix

\[ \left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 6 \\ 0 & 2 & 5 & -4 \\ 2 & 5 & -1 & 27 \end{array} \right) \]
2

Zero out \(x\) in \(R_3\) (\(R_3 = R_3 - 2R_1\))

\[ \left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 6 \\ 0 & 2 & 5 & -4 \\ 0 & 3 & -3 & 15 \end{array} \right) \]
3

Normalize Row 2 (\(R_2 = \frac{1}{2}R_2\))

\[ \left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 6 \\ 0 & 1 & 2.5 & -2 \\ 0 & 3 & -3 & 15 \end{array} \right) \]
4

Eliminate Below Pivot (\(R_3 = R_3 - 3R_2\))

\[ \left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 6 \\ 0 & 1 & 2.5 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & -10.5 & 21 \end{array} \right) \]
5

Normalize Row 3 (\(R_3 = \frac{R_3}{-10.5}\))

\[ \left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 6 \\ 0 & 1 & 2.5 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -2 \end{array} \right) \]
6

Final Back-Substitution

\[ \left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & 0 & 5 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -2 \end{array} \right) \]
Final Solution Vector
x=5, y=3, z=-2
